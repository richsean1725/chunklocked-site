The Loop

One chunk. The rest is war.

Chunklocked flips survival on its head: your land is sacred, everyone else's is a battlefield you can't even dig in.

01

Claim

Spawn with one free claimed chunk in a random wild spot. Inside it, only you can build, mine, and open chests. It's your vault, your farm, your fortress — 16×16 of absolute law.

02

Defend

Step outside and it's lawless wilderness: full PvP, walk-through terrain, and zero block breaking. No creeper holes, no cobble monsters — just you, your gear, and whoever's hunting you.

03

Profit

Sell to a shop that buys 1,354 different items, flip the auction house, then buy more chunks. Past 10, land prices index to the entire server's money supply — an economy that can't inflate away.

Before You Spawn

Quick answers.

How do chunk claims actually work?+
You spawn with one free claimed chunk in a random wild location. Inside chunks you own, only you can build, mine, and open chests. Buy more with in-game money — past 10 chunks the price is indexed to the entire server's money supply, and claiming next to another player's land costs up to 100× more.
What happens outside my claim?+
Everything outside claims is lawless wilderness: you can walk anywhere, PvP is always on, and nobody can break or place blocks. That means no griefing your landscape — but also nowhere to hide.
Can my base get griefed or robbed?+
Not inside your chunks — building, mining, and chest access are locked to you. The server is anti-dupe and anti-cheat hardened with CoreProtect rollback on top. Found a dupe? Report it and get a reward instead of a ban hammer.
Is there chunk upkeep? What if I go inactive?+
Yes — chunks carry a small upkeep cost that acts as a money sink and keeps ghost-town claims from hoarding the map. Casual players cover it easily; empires need real income to hold their borders.
How do I get relics?+
Relics drop from playing: mob kills, fishing, mining, farming and bosses — and some relics are source-locked to one activity only. Bloodmoons double your odds, bosses have guaranteed floors (the Plunderer King always drops Legendary+), and free crates roll every 2 hours of playtime.
How do I reach the Nether and The End?+
Nether: build a portal and claim a nether plot on the other side. The End: perform the Void Compact ritual — 12 ender pearls plus a $2,500 tribute. The void doesn't do free trials.
Can I play on Bedrock or an older version?+
Yes. Bedrock (phones, consoles, Win10/11) joins through Geyser, and Java clients from 1.8 to latest connect via ViaVersion. Everyone plays in the same world: play.chunklocked.com
Is it pay-to-win?+
Ranks (VIP, MVP, LEGEND) give daily crate and coin kits plus queue priority — a head start on the day, not a bypass of the economy. Land pricing, earn softcaps and upkeep apply to everyone equally.
